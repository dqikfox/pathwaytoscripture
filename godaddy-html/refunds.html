Refund Policy

Last Updated: 30 June 2026

Physical Products

Requests for refunds on physical goods must be made within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original condition unless faulty or damaged on arrival.

Digital Products and Event Access

Digital downloads and access rights are generally non-refundable once delivered, except where required by consumer law or where access fails due to a verified service error.

How to Request a Refund

Contact support with your order number, reason for request, and relevant details. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible.

Processing Times

Approved refunds are typically processed within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your payment provider and financial institution.
