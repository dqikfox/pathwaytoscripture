About Us

Pathway to Scripture is a Catholic Bible study community devoted to helping believers of every background grow in knowledge and love of God's Word.

Our sessions are designed to be warm, welcoming, and firmly rooted in the Catholic tradition. Whether you are new to Scripture or looking to deepen your understanding, we provide guided studies, thoughtful discussion, and practical application for everyday life.

We believe that the Bible is living and active — a pathway that leads us closer to Christ, to the Church, and to one another. Through prayerful study, fellowship, and the rich heritage of Catholic teaching, we seek to equip every participant with confidence and clarity.
