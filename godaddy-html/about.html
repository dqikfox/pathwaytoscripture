About Pathway to Scripture

Pathway to Scripture is a Catholic Bible study community created to help believers of every background grow closer to God through Scripture.

Founded by Gabby Haddad, our sessions blend faithful Catholic teaching with practical, everyday application. Whether you are new to the Bible or have studied it for years, you will find a welcoming place here.

We believe that understanding Scripture is not just an academic exercise — it is a pathway to a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ, enriched by prayer, tradition, and the community of believers.

What We Offer

  • Guided group Bible study sessions
  • Catholic teaching rooted in Sacred Tradition and Scripture
  • Opportunities for prayer, fellowship, and personal growth
  • Resources and materials to support your faith journey
